About the challenge

Not just coding. Real impact.

HackVertex – Ideas to Impact is an open-innovation hackathon organized by the Department of Computer Engineering, Bajaj Institute of Technology, Wardha.

The hackathon gives students a platform to identify real-world problems and turn their ideas into practical technology-driven solutions. Participants can work across Web Development, Mobile Applications, AI/ML, Automation, IoT, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Blockchain, and other emerging technologies.

The focus is not just on coding, but on problem-solving, creativity, innovation, teamwork, and real-world impact.

What to submit

Ship a working prototype

Participants must develop a working prototype or functional proof-of-concept addressing a meaningful problem or opportunity. Projects should preferably have a clear college, academic, student, or real-world use case.

Each team should submit:

  1. Project Title
  2. Problem Statement – What problem are you solving?
  3. Solution Description – How does your solution address the problem?
  4. Technology Stack – Technologies, frameworks, APIs, AI models, etc. used.
  5. Working Prototype / Demo
  6. Source Code / Repository Link
  7. Project Presentation / Documentation
  8. Demo Video – if applicable

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate?

HackVertex is open to college students.

What is the team size?

Each team should have 3–4 members.

Do I get a problem statement?

No — it is an open-innovation hackathon. You choose a problem that matters to you.

Which technologies can I use?

Any. Web, mobile, AI/ML, automation, IoT, data science, cybersecurity, blockchain, and other emerging technologies are all welcome.

Who organizes HackVertex?

The Department of Computer Engineering, Bajaj Institute of Technology, Wardha.
